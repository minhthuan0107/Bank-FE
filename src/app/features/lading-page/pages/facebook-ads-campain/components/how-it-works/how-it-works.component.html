How does it work?

Setting up your virtual credit card (VCC) with Finup takes just minutes — and it’s designed for seamless use on top advertising platforms like Meta Ads Manager, Google, or TikTok.

01

1. Create an account

Sign up quickly — no long forms or delays. Get instant access to your dashboard and start using a VCC without waiting for approvals.

02

2. Generate a VCC

Choose from Visa or Mastercard and instantly generate a VCC with high compatibility across payment methods on ad platforms. You can choose from 20+ BIN options.

03

3. Fund & Link

Top up your card with crypto or traditional payments, then link it as a payment method in your ads manager.

04

4. Launch & Monitor

Run campaigns immediately with high limits and full control. Monitor spending in real-time, receive instant alerts, and manage everything from your dashboard.
