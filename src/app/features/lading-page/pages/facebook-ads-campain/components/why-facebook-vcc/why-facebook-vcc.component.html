Facebook Ads VCC

Why use a virtual credit card for Facebook Advertising?

Virtual cards help advertisers separate budgets, reduce payment risks, and manage Facebook ad spending with more control and visibility.

Separate ad budgets

Create dedicated VCCs for different Facebook ad accounts, campaigns, or clients, so each budget stays clean and easy to track.

Reduce payment risk

Avoid using one main card across multiple ad accounts. A virtual card helps limit exposure and keeps your primary payment source safer.

Create cards instantly

Generate ready-to-use virtual cards quickly and start assigning them to your Facebook advertising workflows without waiting for physical cards.

Track spending clearly

Monitor card transactions separately, making it easier to understand which campaign, account, or client is spending money.

Control each card

Manage each VCC independently for campaign spending, payment control, and easier operation across multiple advertising accounts.

Scale ad operations

For teams running multiple Facebook ad accounts, VCCs make payment management more organized, flexible, and scalable.

With virtual cards, Facebook advertising payments become easier to separate, monitor, and control — especially when managing multiple campaigns or accounts.
