What is a Virtual Card for TikTok Ads?

A virtual card for TikTok Ads is a digital payment card created for advertising transactions on TikTok. Instead of using one main card for every ad account, you can create dedicated cards to separate budgets, manage campaign payments, and keep ad spending easier to track.

Budget Control

Assign different cards to different TikTok ad accounts, clients, or campaign groups.

Payment Separation

Keep TikTok ad payments separated instead of mixing every account on one main card.

Digital card, real payments

A virtual card works like a regular payment card, but it is created online and can be used for digital billing. For TikTok Ads, it helps you connect a dedicated card to your ad payment method without relying on a physical card.

Built for ad account management

When you manage many TikTok campaigns, clients, or ad accounts, one shared card can become hard to control. Virtual cards let you organize payments by account, campaign type, or team workflow.

Cleaner billing records

Each card can represent a specific advertising purpose. This makes it easier to review transactions, compare spending, and understand where your TikTok Ads budget is going.
